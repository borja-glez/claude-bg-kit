# Resuming

Read from forge:run (`## Resuming` in `SKILL.md`). `FORGE` and `<id>` mean what that skill says. `<WORK>` is a task's work folder (`references/task-steps.md`): in an execution, its `work` in `FORGE swarm status`.

1. `FORGE last --task <id>`: `path` and `phase` (the whole last phase event, with its `brief` number `n`).
2. `FORGE last --task <id>-<n>`: the last verdict (with its review `dir`) and the last round of that brief.
3. Continue from there; the phase event's `base` is the `BASE` for `redo` (in an execution, after a conflict retry, `<WORK>/base-<n>.json` holds the base the reviews moved to: when it exists, its `base` is the `<base>` of the task's next steps); never redo finished work. If the last verdict already covers the newest review dir, do not close that round again: follow the `next` of the last round. Run `FORGE review-close` only for a round review-prep prepared that has no verdict yet (on Large's final review, `FORGE verdict` only for a final dir that has none).
4. In an execution (`FORGE swarm status` shows it active and you are its coordinator; the SessionStart context says "Resume it with /forge:run → Resume", or you came back with `claude --resume`): renew your lease, then run `FORGE swarm start` with the same `--project` and `--session`, `--hook-session <CSID>`, plus the `--mode` and `--parallel` that `FORGE swarm status` shows (and `--kind ephemeral` for an ephemeral run, which then follows `references/ephemeral.md` → Resume) (it resumes without changing anything but the binding: this Claude session becomes the one the Stop hook steps, also after a `/clear`; without them `start` takes the mode and N from `.forge/config.yaml`). If it exits 2 saying the execution is bound to another Claude session, tell the user what it says and stop: if that conversation is still open, go on from it; if it was closed or crashed, `claude --resume` it to go on at once, or wait about 12 min from its last step, then `/forge:run --resume` again. Reconcile each task in `running`: a subagents task as before, reading its stage, agent, round and worktree from `FORGE swarm status` and its review dirs (`<WORK>/reviews-*`): an agent that is not running any more is respawned for that step; a verdict already written is not recomputed. A sessions task by ListAgents: a live `wp-<project>-<T>` session is left alone; a dead one gets the `relaunch` action of the next `tick`. Then go to `references/execution.md` step 2. An execution started in auto (`FORGE swarm status` shows `autonomy: auto`) keeps deciding alone as `references/autonomy.md` says.
   **Takeover.** A `FORGE swarm start` that exits 2 naming `swarm takeover` (another session coordinates the run) comes here too. A new session asked to go on with the execution ("sigue con la ejecución") while another session coordinates it: get `SID` (`/waypoint:work` §0 Setup items 1–4), then `FORGE swarm takeover --session <SID> --hook-session <CSID>`. `took: true` → register as coordinator (`sessions/<SID>` as `references/execution.md` step 1, with its lease; `--hook-session` already bound this session, so its Stop hook steps you), claim again with a versioned write the `owner` of each task it lists as `running` whose `executor` (`FORGE swarm status`) is `subagents` (`FORGE swarm task <T> --lease <NOW + 10 min>`, then one ArtifactData `batch` with `update state/T` `{owner: SID}` pinned to its version and, after it, the `publish` that command printed, without `if_version`: forge rebuilt `exec` with this coordinator), publish `meta/run` (`references/execution.md` step 5) and go on from step 4 above. `took: "already"` → you already coordinate it: step 4 above. `took: false` → tell the user who coordinates it (`previous`, and its `liveness`) and join as a manual member (`/waypoint:work`, which sends you to `references/manual-member.md`).

## /forge:run --resume

`/forge:run --resume` takes this checkout's execution up in this conversation: the band's **Resume** on an execution sends it, and so does the person when the SessionStart text of an execution this conversation does not coordinate offers it. It never starts anything without saying so.
1. `FORGE swarm status`. No execution, or one with `closedAt`: say there is nothing to resume, and stop.
2. **Bind first**, as `references/execution.md` step 1 does: a conversation that came back after `/clear` holds no binding. `node "<WP>" bind <plan> --via forge-run --claude <CSID> --seat <coordinator> --resume`, with `<WP>` = `FORGE waypoint-cli`, `<plan>` = `docs/waypoint/<project>/plan.yaml` and the `project` and `coordinator` that `FORGE swarm status` shows (`--resume` takes that seat when the conversation that held it ended with a task in progress); exits 3 and 4 as step 1 says. `SID` = its `sid`. An ephemeral run binds only when the checkout's project has a roadmap, with that project's plan (`references/ephemeral.md` step 2); without one, `SID` = its `coordinator`.
3. Active (its coordinator's conversation ended or went silent, or no session drives it: `unbound`): step 4 above with `--session <SID>` (an ephemeral run: `references/ephemeral.md` → Resume). A `FORGE swarm start` that exits 2 saying the execution is bound to another Claude session means that conversation may still step the run: tell the person what it says (go on from it if it is open, `claude --resume` it if it was closed or crashed, or wait about 12 min from its last step and `/forge:run --resume` again), and stop. One that exits 2 naming `swarm takeover` (another SID coordinates it): **Takeover** above, with this `SID`.
4. When `hookSession` is not `<CSID>` and `coordinator.liveness` is not `stopped`, the conversation that coordinates it is still on it (finishing it, or waiting on the person): tell the person to go on from it (`claude --resume <hookSession>` if it was closed or crashed), and stop. Ended and not closed: tell the person why it stopped (`stopReason`, and `stallReason` when it stalled), then:
   - `stalled`: `FORGE swarm retry <T>` for each task in `conflict` or `blocked` the person wants retried revives it, then step 3 here; with none, nothing is retried;
   - `finished` without a merge: the final review and `FORGE swarm merge` of `references/execution.md` step 7;
   - `stopped` or `budget`: a stopped run is not resumed. Start a new one for the same plan with `references/execution.md` step 1 (an ephemeral run: a new one for the requests left, as `references/ephemeral.md` says): its `swarm start` replaces the old one (`replaced`), and the tasks the old one released start again on their `task/<T>` branches, whose earlier commits are reviewed with them (`prior`).
5. Whatever the person decides not to resume, `/forge:stop` closes.
